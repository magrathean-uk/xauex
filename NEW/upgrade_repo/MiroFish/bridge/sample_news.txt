Gold Market Analysis - January 2025

Federal Reserve Policy:
The Federal Reserve signaled a potential pause in interest rate hikes at its latest meeting.
Fed Chair Jerome Powell noted that inflation is "moving in the right direction" but emphasized
data-dependency. Markets are pricing in a 65% probability of a rate cut by March 2025.

Geopolitical Tensions:
Ongoing conflicts in the Middle East continue to support safe-haven demand for gold.
Recent escalation between regional powers has driven a 2% surge in gold prices over the past week.
NATO allies have increased defense spending commitments.

US Dollar:
The US Dollar Index (DXY) dropped 0.8% this week to 103.2, providing tailwinds for gold.
Analysts at Goldman Sachs project further dollar weakness if the Fed pivots to rate cuts.

Institutional Flows:
Gold ETF holdings (GLD, IAU) saw $1.2 billion in net inflows over the past two weeks,
the largest since September 2024. Central banks continue net gold purchases,
with China and India leading Q4 accumulation.

Technical Levels:
Gold is trading at $2,685 per ounce, above its 200-day moving average of $2,450.
Key resistance at $2,700, support at $2,640.

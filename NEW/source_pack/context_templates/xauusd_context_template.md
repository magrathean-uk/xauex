# XAUUSD context template

## 1. Central-bank and rates
- What changed in Fed communication?
- Did CPI, payrolls or PCE alter real-rate expectations?
- What does FedWatch imply now?

## 2. USD and macro regime
- Is the USD strengthening or weakening?
- Is the market in risk-on or risk-off mode?

## 3. Gold-specific flows
- ETF inflows or outflows?
- Central-bank reserve headlines?
- LBMA or WGC liquidity context?

## 4. Positioning
- What does CFTC imply about crowded positioning?

## 5. Geopolitics
- Which events increase or reduce safe-haven demand?

## 6. Decision summary
- Base case: BUY / SELL / HOLD
- Key invalidation
- Why now?

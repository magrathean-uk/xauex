# WTI context template

## 1. Inventories and demand
- Latest EIA crude, gasoline and distillate changes
- Refinery utilization and product demand

## 2. Supply and outages
- OPEC and non-OPEC guidance
- Pipeline, shipping or production disruptions
- Weather or hurricane impacts

## 3. Macro overlay
- USD direction and global growth narrative
- Any policy or sanctions changes affecting oil flows?

## 4. Positioning
- Is the market crowded long or short according to CFTC?

## 5. Decision summary
- Base case: BUY / SELL / HOLD
- Key catalyst for follow-through
- Main reason the trade could fail

# WTI source list

| Tier | Name | Kind | Auto | Why | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report | html | yes | The WPSR is the anchor public inventory release for WTI, especially crude, gasoline, distillates and refinery utilization. | https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/ |
| 1 | EIA Today in Energy RSS | rss | yes | Timely official energy-market analysis is useful context for inventories, demand and infrastructure trends. | https://www.eia.gov/rss/todayinenergy.xml |
| 1 | EIA press releases RSS | rss | yes | Captures official announcements, outlook changes and methodology notes that can reset market expectations. | https://www.eia.gov/rss/press_rss.xml |
| 1 | EIA gasoline and diesel RSS | rss | yes | Downstream pricing and crack-spread context help the swarm reason about refinery demand and product tightness. | https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/includes/gas_diesel_rss.xml |
| 1 | EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook | html | yes | The STEO provides official demand, supply and price outlook revisions that shape the medium-term oil narrative. | https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/outlook.php |
| 2 | EIA API documentation | api | manual | Best source for structured inventory, production and refinery data once you add series-specific adapters. | https://www.eia.gov/opendata/documentation.php |
| 2 | EIA open data browser | html | manual | Helpful for discovering the exact series you want to bind into the oil context pipeline. | https://www.eia.gov/opendata/index.php/browser/browser/faqs.php |
| 2 | CFTC Commitments of Traders | html | yes | Positioning helps detect crowded oil trades and whether inventory reactions may extend or mean revert. | https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm |
| 2 | NOAA National Hurricane Center tropical weather outlook RSS | rss | yes | Storm threats can materially affect Gulf production, refining and shipping infrastructure. | https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gtwo.xml |
| 2 | NOAA NHC Atlantic outlook RSS | rss | yes | Adds Atlantic storm bulletins and updates that often matter for oil more than broad macro headlines do. | https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/index-at.xml |
| 2 | OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report | html | manual | OPEC demand, supply and balance revisions shape the global oil narrative and can move front-month crude. | https://www.opec.org/monthly-oil-market-report.html |
| 2 | IEA Oil Market Report | html | manual | A high-signal global oil balance source, especially for demand revisions and inventory interpretation. | https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-product/oil-market-report-omr |
| 3 | MINING.com oil sands RSS | rss | yes | Useful for Canada-heavy supply and infrastructure headlines that can bleed into North American crude narratives. | https://mining.com/tag/oil-sands/feed/ |
